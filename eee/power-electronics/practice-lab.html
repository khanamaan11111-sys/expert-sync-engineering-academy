Converter & Power Calculator

Check ideal converter relationships, electrical power and efficiency. Change the inputs and compare the results with your lesson calculations. All calculations run in your browser; no sign-in is required.

1. Ideal DC–DC converter

Select a topology and calculate.
Ideal continuous-conduction, steady-state equations: buck Vout = D × Vin; boost Vout = Vin / (1 − D). These are not hardware design ratings.

2. Electrical power

P = V × I
For DC voltage and current. AC real power may additionally depend on power factor and waveform.

3. Converter efficiency

η = Pout / Pin × 100%
Use input and output power averaged consistently at the same operating condition. Values above 100% indicate inconsistent inputs or measurements for a passive converter.

  3. Power: 28 V at 9 A. Find output power.

Important safety note

This educational calculator omits semiconductor drops, losses, switching ripple, control-loop behavior, thermal constraints, device ratings and protection. Do not use its results alone to select components, build hardware or connect equipment to mains, PV strings or high-energy batteries.
