EEE / POWER ELECTRONICS

10-Level Power Electronics Roadmap

A structured curriculum outline from fundamentals to converter design, simulation, renewable energy applications and career preparation. All 10 levels now have published educational lessons or projects, with practice and quizzes.

LEVEL 01 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Electrical & Electronics Fundamentals

Circuit laws, AC/DC, components, measurement and electrical safety.

LEVEL 02 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Power Semiconductor Devices

Diodes, MOSFETs, IGBTs, switching characteristics and gate driving.

LEVEL 03 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Rectifiers & AC–DC Conversion

Uncontrolled and controlled rectifiers, filtering and ripple.

LEVEL 04 • LESSON AVAILABLE

DC–DC Converters

Buck, boost, buck–boost converters, duty cycle and efficiency.

LEVEL 05 • LESSON AVAILABLE

DC–AC Inverters

Single-phase and three-phase inverters, PWM and harmonics.

LEVEL 06 • LESSON AVAILABLE

MATLAB & Simulink Practice

Build basic converter models and examine voltage/current waveforms.

LEVEL 07 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Control & Feedback

Open-loop vs closed-loop control, sensing, PI control and stability basics.

LEVEL 08 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Renewable Energy Applications

PV systems, MPPT concepts, battery interfaces and grid connection overview.

LEVEL 09 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Practical Design Project

Define specifications, model a converter, evaluate results and document limitations.

LEVEL 10 • LESSON AVAILABLE

Portfolio & Interview Readiness

Present design decisions, calculations, simulations and technical interview topics.
