CIVIL / QUANTITY SURVEYOR / LEVEL 6

BOQ & Estimation

Turn measured quantities into a structured, traceable construction estimate.

0%
LESSON 01

BOQ Fundamentals

OBJECTIVE

Understand BOQ purpose, structure and traceability.

A BOQ organizes measured work into descriptions, units and quantities for systematic pricing and review. Professional BOQs must follow project documents, contract requirements and the applicable method of measurement.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Create a 10-item BOQ skeleton: Item No., Description, Unit, Quantity, Rate, Amount.

LESSON 02

Take-Off to BOQ

OBJECTIVE

Transfer measured quantities while preserving an audit trail.

The take-off is the measurement record; the BOQ presents measured work in a structured form. Reconcile every transferred quantity to its source.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Transfer five Level 3 quantities and record their drawing/take-off references.

LESSON 03

Descriptions & Units

OBJECTIVE

Write clear item descriptions and use appropriate measurement units.

Descriptions should identify the work sufficiently for the exercise and align with specifications. Common units include m, m², m³, kg/t and number, depending on applicable rules.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Write descriptions and units for 10 building work items.

LESSON 04

Work Sections

OBJECTIVE

Organize BOQ items into logical construction sections.

Items may be grouped into earthwork, concrete, masonry, finishes and other project-defined sections. Consistent grouping supports checking and summaries.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Group 15 practice items into appropriate sections.

LESSON 05

Rates & Pricing Inputs

OBJECTIVE

Use controlled pricing inputs and document source and date.

Real estimates require current, location/project-appropriate and approved inputs. Training values must be clearly labelled illustrative, never presented as live market rates.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Add Rate Source, Date and Assumption Status columns to a practice estimate.

LESSON 06

Amount Calculations

OBJECTIVE

Calculate item amounts and totals accurately.

The basic item calculation is Quantity × Rate. Use formulas, consistent currency formats and project-defined rounding. Do not manually overwrite calculated amounts.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Build formulas for 10 items and a grand total.

LESSON 07

Estimate Summary

OBJECTIVE

Create section totals and a concise estimate abstract.

A summary should reconcile to the detailed BOQ. Any allowances or additions should be separately identified and used only when the exercise/project defines them.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Create section totals for Earthwork, Concrete, Masonry and Finishes and reconcile them.

LESSON 08

Checking & Reconciliation

OBJECTIVE

Detect quantity, unit, formula, transfer and pricing-input errors.

Check drawing revision, duplicates, missing items, units, quantities, formulas, rate sources and totals. Reconciliation is a control, not proof that technical measurement is correct.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Apply a 10-point QA checklist to your BOQ.

LESSON 09

Revision Control

OBJECTIVE

Track estimate changes transparently.

Record revision number/date, reason for change and affected items. Preserve prior assumptions and follow project document-control procedures.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Create Rev 00 and Rev 01 of a small estimate and prepare a change log.

LESSON 10

Level 6 Project

OBJECTIVE

Produce a connected training BOQ and estimate.

Combine drawing references, take-off, Excel measurement, BOQ, controlled training rates, summary and QA. Another reviewer should be able to understand the calculation trail.

PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT

Complete a minimum 15-item training BOQ and estimate, including assumptions and unresolved queries.
