LESSON 01

Define Your QS Job Target

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Translate training into a realistic role target.

Identify entry-level or junior QS/estimation roles that match your demonstrated skills. Read each vacancy separately because titles and requirements vary by employer and country. Separate required qualifications from preferred skills.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Create a target-role sheet: title, location, required skills, software, experience requirement and evidence you currently have.

LESSON 02

Build a Credible QS CV

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Present skills and project evidence without overstating experience.

Your CV should distinguish education, employment, training and academy simulations. Use specific evidence such as quantity take-off, BOQ preparation, Excel and CAD practice. Never present the Level 9 simulation as paid client experience.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Draft a one- or two-page role-focused CV and map every technical claim to evidence.

LESSON 03

Build Your QS Portfolio

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Package work so a recruiter or interviewer can inspect it quickly.

Select clean evidence from the Level 9 project: project brief, take-off extracts, measurement workbook screenshots, CAD checks, BOQ, rate analysis, cost-control output and QA. Remove confidential information from any real-world material.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Create a portfolio cover, contents page, project summary, 6–10 evidence pages and skills-evidence matrix.

LESSON 04

Technical Interview Preparation

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Prepare to explain QS concepts rather than memorize definitions.

Practice explaining measurement, drawings, BOQ, rate analysis, estimation, billing, variations and cost control. Good answers state the principle, workflow, checks and project-specific limitations.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Prepare spoken answers to 25 technical QS questions and support at least five with examples from your academy project.

LESSON 05

Drawing & Take-Off Practical Test

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Practice the type of task that can reveal real measurement ability.

Use a controlled practice drawing or supplied exercise. Check revision/units first, identify scope, measure systematically, state assumptions and perform a self-check. Accuracy and traceability matter more than speed alone.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Run a timed practice: review a small drawing and produce a traceable take-off for selected items.

LESSON 06

Excel & BOQ Practical Test

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Demonstrate spreadsheet competence under interview conditions.

Practice formulas, references, structured tables, sorting/filtering, quantity calculations, BOQ amounts, summaries and error checks. Avoid hard-coded totals where formulas should be used.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Complete a timed Excel exercise that converts supplied quantities and rates into a checked BOQ summary.

LESSON 07

Scenario & Commercial Questions

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Respond professionally when information is incomplete or disputed.

QS interviews may test how you handle missing drawings, quantity differences, unsupported claims, rate changes or variation status. Explain that you would verify documents, record assumptions/queries, follow authority levels and preserve an audit trail.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Prepare responses to 15 scenarios, including drawing conflict, unsupported quantity, rate discrepancy and unapproved change.

LESSON 08

HR & Communication Interview

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Connect your background to the role clearly and concisely.

Prepare a short introduction, motivation for QS work, strengths, development areas, teamwork examples and learning story. Keep answers truthful and job-relevant; technical training is evidence of learning, not a substitute for employment experience.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Record or rehearse a 60-second introduction plus answers to 10 common HR questions.

LESSON 09

Applications & Interview Tracking

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Run the job search as a measurable process.

Track employer, role, location, application date, source, status, interview stages, feedback and follow-up. Tailor the CV to documented vacancy requirements rather than mass-claiming every skill.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Create an application tracker and define a weekly application/review routine.

LESSON 10

Final Career Readiness Review

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Audit evidence before claiming job readiness.

Completion alone does not guarantee employment or professional competence. Review whether you can independently explain and demonstrate the core tasks, identify limitations, use controlled documents and present your portfolio credibly.

🎯 JOB-READINESS TASK

Complete the final checklist, repeat weak practical tests, and prepare a 5-minute walkthrough of your Level 9 project.
